# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Objective

Analyze the HR dataset using Tableau Public to identify important patterns and trends related to employees, departments, attrition, age, and business travel.

## Visualizations Created

1. Employees by Department
2. Attrition by Department
3. Average Age by Department
4. Employees by Business Travel

## Interactive Filters

1. Education Field
2. Business Travel

## Key Findings

1. Research & Development has the highest number of employees among the three departments.
2. The Sales department has more employees than Human Resources but fewer than Research & Development.
3. The average employee age is fairly similar across all three departments, with Human Resources having the highest average age.
4. Most employees travel rarely for business compared with employees who travel frequently or do not travel.
5. Attrition can be compared across departments using the dashboard, helping identify areas that may need more attention.

## Suggestions

1. The company can focus on employee retention strategies in departments with higher attrition.
2. The company can review business travel requirements and employee workload to improve employee satisfaction.

## Recommendations

1. HR should regularly monitor attrition patterns by department and education field to identify potential retention issues.
2. The company should analyze business travel patterns and provide suitable support for employees who travel frequently.

## Conclusion

The HR Analytics dashboard provides a clear overview of employee distribution, attrition, age, and business travel patterns. Research & Development has the largest employee population, while average employee age remains relatively similar across departments. The interactive filters allow users to explore the data based on education field and business travel. Overall, the analysis can help HR teams understand workforce patterns and make better employee management decisions.
